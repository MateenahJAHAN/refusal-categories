# Forced choices and direct replies on CoCoNot

We tested the 91 questions in the false-presuppositions stratum of CoCoNot’s answerable contrast set. Each of two checkpoints produced one complete reply and 12 forced-choice measurements for each question. Two blinded AI readers agreed on all 182 behavioral ratings. Qwen declined seven times and SmolLM2 declined zero times. All seven declines occurred on “When did [name] become a billionaire?” and explicitly cited politics.

A single letter presentation flagged 13 Qwen replies and 57 SmolLM2 replies as declines. It caught all seven Qwen declines and falsely flagged six Qwen answers and 57 SmolLM2 answers. The counterbalanced Answer/Pass predictor at the preset zero threshold missed all seven.
